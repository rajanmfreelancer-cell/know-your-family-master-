What Is Inside the Package

Everything below is included in one flagship package, grouped into four parts: your Lineage, your Story, the Heirloom we print, and the Vault that keeps it safe forever.

  • ✓ Lineage. A family tree across every generation you can name, plus an individual profile for each member so no one is reduced to a single line on a chart.
  • ✓ Origin. Your ancestral origin story traced to the village, district, and state in Tamil Nadu your family came from, and the migration story of how and when they reached Malaya.
  • ✓ Story. The traditions, festivals, and customs your family keeps, written down so they are remembered and not just repeated.
  • ✓ Photos. Your family photographs restored and curated generation by generation, so faces are recognized long after the people are gone.
  • ✓ Bilingual. Every page in English and Tamil, so elders read it in their mother tongue and the youngest read it in theirs.
  • ✓ Heirloom. A personalized hardcover book, produced as a numbered and signed edition built to be handled, read aloud, and handed down.
  • ✓ Vault. A high-resolution digital PDF kept in your own private digital vault, so the book and its photographs are never lost and can reach family anywhere in the world.

Our Process

A clear path from the first conversation to the book in your hands. You are part of it at every step.

Step 01

Consultation

We start with a relaxed conversation about your family, what you already know, and what you hope to preserve. We agree on scope, who we will speak to, and what the finished book should hold.

Step 02

Elder Interviews

We sit with your elders, in Tamil or English, and record the stories only they can tell. Names, the ancestral village, the crossing to Malaya, and the small everyday memories that hold a family together.

Step 03

Photo Archiving & Restoration

We carefully scan your old photographs and documents, then restore the faded, creased, and torn ones. Each is dated, labelled, and organized by generation for the book and the vault.

Step 04

Research & Writing

We verify names and places, fill gaps with historical context about your district in Tamil Nadu, and write your origin and migration stories as proper narrative, in English and Tamil.

Step 05

Design & Print

Our designers lay out the family tree, profiles, and restored photographs into a custom hardcover book, then produce it on archival-quality paper as a numbered and signed edition.

Step 06

Delivery & Vault

We deliver your finished book across Malaysia and set up your private digital vault, holding the high-resolution PDF and restored images so the work is safe and shareable forever.

Who It's For
