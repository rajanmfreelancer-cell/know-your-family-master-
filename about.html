The Origin Story

In the early 2000s, our founder, a lawyer, was travelling across Malaysia and nearby countries for his legal work. Along the way he built relationships with Tamil families and communities, sharing meals, listening to memories, and earning trust that took years to grow.

One day, two of his close friends discovered they shared the same grandfather's name. Curious, they dug deeper, and found out they were relatives who had never known each other existed.

"That moment stayed with him. How many Tamil families had lost their connections without even realising it?"

He knew the roots were there, buried in memory, in old photographs, in stories passed down imperfectly. But life moved fast, and no one was recording them. For more than 20 years he carried that mission close, through his legal career, through every Tamil family he met, through every story he heard. That knowledge never left him.

Today, Know Your Family begins a new chapter. Built on two decades of community trust and cultural understanding, we are returning with modern technology, careful digital systems, and a dedicated team, to give every Tamil family in Malaysia a permanent way to find, preserve, and pass down their heritage.

In print. Online. Forever.

Our Mission

Most Tamil families in Malaysia carry their story in fragments. A grandmother remembers the village in Tamil Nadu. An uncle keeps the migration story. A faded photograph holds a face no one can name. When an elder passes, those fragments often go with them, and a family loses the thread that ties one generation to the next.

Our mission is to make sure that never has to happen. We record the voices of your elders while they are here to share them. We restore the photographs before they fade further. We trace the lineage that links cousins who have drifted apart. Then we hold it all in two places that last: a hardcover Heritage Book your family can pass from hand to hand, and a private digital vault you can open from anywhere in the world.

For the Tamil diaspora, this is more than a record. It is proof of where you come from, kept safe for the children who will want to know.

How We Work

The journey from a living memory to a finished Heritage Book moves through three careful stages.

  • 1 Interview.  We sit with your elders and record their stories, memories, and timelines, in Tamil and English, at a pace that lets them remember in full.
  • 2 Archive.  We digitise and restore your old photographs and documents, then research your lineage to connect the generations into one clear family tree.

Why Tamil Heritage, Why Now

Each generation born in Malaysia stands a little further from the village their family left in Tamil Nadu. The grandparents who made the crossing carried the language, the customs, and the names by heart. Their grandchildren grow up between worlds, proud to be Tamil, yet unsure who came before them or how the family found its way here.

This is the diaspora identity gap, and it widens quietly. When an elder passes without being recorded, a family does not just lose a person. It loses the only living link to an entire branch of its past. Once that voice is gone, no amount of money or technology can bring it back.

That is why now matters. The elders who remember are with us today, and the tools to preserve them have never been better. Capturing this heritage is not nostalgia, it is a gift to the children who will one day ask, "Where do we come from?"
