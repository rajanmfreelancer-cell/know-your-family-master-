This is where the Tamil diaspora comes to understand where they came from. Our stories trace the migration of Tamil families across the Indian Ocean to Malaysia and beyond, explain how to research your genealogy and ancestral village, and explore the customs, language, and beliefs that still shape who we are. Whether you are documenting your grandparents' memories or starting to map your family tree, you will find practical guidance and cultural context here, told with care for the heritage behind every name.
